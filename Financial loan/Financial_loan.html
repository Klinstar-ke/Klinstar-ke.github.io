    Analysis of the dashboard.

    This dashboard presents various charts with each chart giving a clear insight and information of the loan perfomance. These includes KPI cards, donut charts , stacked bar plots, geographical maps and line graphs.

    Key KPI Cards

    The KPI cards represents deep details on the various metrics as shown below:
    • Total number of applicants 38.8K: This represents a strong demand for loans within the institution.
    • Total Loan Amount lend:$436M This indicates that the institution has a huge lending portfolio.
    • Avg Interest Rate of 12.0%: This interest rate can be interpreted to as a moderate lending risk.
    • Total Payment made of $473M This figure shows that the portfolio is making a profit due to the interest earned in comparison to the amount lend.
    • A 13.8% Default Rate indicating that its manageable but a notable credit risk.
    • 32k loan settlement: Out of the 38.8k loan applicants, majority of them have successfully repaid their loans.
    • We have a thousand loans that are active out of the 38.8K while the others have been charged off.

      • Insights:The KpI cards proves that the business is healthy overall due to the high repayment levels and profitability. However another insight emerges that we have a significant number of defaults and hence great need for watching out.

    Loan Status Distribution (Donut Chart)

    This donut chart represents the various proportions of the loan status grouped in three categories:
    • Fully paid loan status representing an approx of 80.6% of the entire chart showing dominance.
    • Charged Off status represented by 15.0%
    • Current and active loans represented by a strong4.3%

    Overall insight: This chart shows the strong repayment culture with a few active loans suggesting that the dataset is somehow historical. While the defaults exist, they are somehow contained.

    Loan Amount by Grade

    This chart represents the perfomance of the various grades by their grade. These loan grades include A,B,C,D,E,F and G. Grade B represents the highest loan recording with an approximate of 131M borrowed. This is followed by grades C,A and D while the lowest is grade G with an approximate borrowed amount of 6M.

    Insight:Most lending is concentrated on mid-grade borrowers B and C.

    Analysis by loan purpose

    While there exists various reasons as to why different people borrow money for this datasets shows a clear margin that most people borrow inorder to help resurface existing loans. Other reasons include credit card refinancing, home improvement, small businesses among other things.

    Insights:Most people borrow for debt consolidation rather than new spending. While this is the major reason, this poses an increased risk for the company mostly if the borrowers who are financially strained.

    Monthly Trend

    The line chart shows a steady growth of the lending company showing an upward trend from a 25Min January to 54M by the end of December.

    Insight:Lending activity is increasing over time indicating a potential business growth and seasonal demand.

    Geographic Distribution

    From the regional mappimg of the finance borrowers, we have most loans concentrated in North America and Europe while we have less borrowers from the African region.

    Note: The dashboards contain multiple slicers which give room for further analytics. More analytics can be done in terms of the term, address states, loan status as well as Grade. The dashboard is readily available upon request
